### Results and interpretation

The matched smooth control changed the solution regularity decisively: reference maxima were `max|u_x|=0.927793` and `max|u_xx|=1.228666`, compared with `29.499847` and `757.666568` for the shock-forming trajectory. The refined-grid discrepancy was `1.51e-5`, and the exact-reference feature library recovered Burgers to coefficient error `1.36e-15`.

The smooth control did not restore frozen symbolic recovery under the matched joint-surrogate trajectory. Loose recovery was `0/25` at checkpoints 0, 50, 200, 500, and 1000; strong recovery was `0/25` at every checkpoint. Median coefficient error changed from `1.125248` to `0.886466`. The median transport coefficient moved from `+0.056676` to `-0.629130`, and the median diffusion coefficient from `0.001558` to `0.014596`, so the learned probes moved materially toward the target but remained outside the loose transport threshold and retained large spurious terms (`0.805139` median at epoch 1000).

Derivative fidelity improved relative to Phase 23 at every terminal primitive except the temporal derivative comparison is regime-dependent: smooth terminal MSEs were `u=0.168824`, `u_x=0.978805`, `u_xx=299.714`, and `u_t=4.430536`, versus Phase 23 `u=0.031030`, `u_x=3.112945`, `u_xx=2646.867`, and `u_t=0.860947`. Thus the smooth field has much better spatial derivative errors but its joint surrogate still fits the field less closely and has larger temporal residual error. This mixed outcome matters: removing steep gradients improves the second-derivative burden and moves coefficients toward Burgers, but it does not by itself make the tested symbolic optimizer recover the PDE.

The shock hypothesis is therefore weakened as a sufficient explanation and remains plausible as a contributing factor. The result does not prove that sharp gradients are irrelevant; the controls compare one smooth trajectory and one shock-forming trajectory, each conditioned on one joint surrogate path, and the surrogate optimization itself differs because the data differ. The next upstream explanation is surrogate/joint-training fidelity and trajectory conditioning: even on the smooth data, the learned `u_xx` and `u_t` fields remain too inaccurate for robust symbolic recovery. Next controls should compare data-only surrogate fitting against joint fitting on the same smooth trajectory, then localize derivative and feature errors and test whether a better smooth surrogate enables recovery.
